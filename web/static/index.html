Search a library of published, de-identified patient case reports the way you think about a case — look something up by an exact term (a drug, a dose, a lab value, an abbreviation), or describe a whole clinical picture in plain language. One box decides whether your query is best answered by keyword, blended, or meaning-based search, shows you which, and deep-links every match to its source report.

Find precedents for an unusual presentation · see how other clinicians managed a drug or complication · build a quick cohort by specialty, age, or clinical event · pull up similar patients from any result.

Notes come from PMC-Patients, a public corpus of published, de-identified case reports licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0. This is a search demo, not raw EHR and not for clinical use.
Routing, hybrid fusion, snapshots, and the live facet counts are all hev layer gateway features — the app adds no search code of its own.
